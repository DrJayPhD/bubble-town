Load a song (MP3, WAV or M4A). It listens on this device, finds the notes, tempo, key and chords, and writes a piano cover you can read as sheet music, watch as falling notes, or print as a PDF. For the cleanest result add stems too: the vocals stem gives the melody, and the bass and instrumental stems give the chords.
